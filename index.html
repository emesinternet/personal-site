Practical questions

Do you design and build websites?

Yes. I create public-facing sites and internal applications shaped around how your business works.

Does my business need AI?

Often, no. That can be a good thing, because it usually means we can get to a reliable solution faster. Sometimes the answer is yes, but not always in the way you might expect. AI can be useful, but it can also make mistakes. My job is to help you understand where it makes sense and where it does not.

Do you work with non-technical business owners?

Yes. You do not need to be technical to make informed decisions. I explain your options in plain language and make complex technology easier to understand.

Do you only build AI projects?

No. Some of the clearest, most reliable, and scalable solutions do not involve AI. I use the term “Practical AI” because AI is often more machinery than the problem requires.

What do you mean by Practical AI?

Practical AI means I only use it when it improves a real process, reduces work, and is safe enough for your business to trust. AI can produce different or incorrect results, so it is simply not the right tool for every situation.

Can you work with existing software?

Usually, yes. My job is not to start by replacing everything. It is to assess what you already have, explain your options, and find the best path to leave your business in a better position than it was before.
